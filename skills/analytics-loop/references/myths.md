# Myths (do not act on them)

- **Post daily to grow.** Frequency without quality spreads impressions thin. A rhythm you can hold wins.
- **70% retention is the bar.** Retention depends on length and topic. Compare with your own videos.
- **Tags and keyword stuffing rank videos.** Viewer satisfaction does. Tags mainly catch misspellings.
- **There is a best hour to publish.** Distribution runs for weeks. Publish when you can watch the first two hours.
- **The first hour decides everything.** Search and suggested traffic build over weeks; old videos come back.
- **A good CTR is X%.** CTR depends on the source, the audience size and the topic.
- **Shorts feed long-form subscribers.** Different viewers, different behaviour. Treat Shorts as their own format.
- **Longer is always better for watch time.** Only if every minute earns its place. Padding lowers satisfaction.
- **Deleting weak videos helps the channel.** Unlist if you must. Deleting removes data and rarely changes anything.
- **Changing many things at once is faster.** It is slower: you will not know which change worked.
