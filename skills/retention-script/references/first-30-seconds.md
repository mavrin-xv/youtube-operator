# The first 30 seconds

The share of viewers still watching at 0:30 (shown in Studio as intro retention, here called R30) is the first number
that tells you whether the package and the opening agree.

## Rules

- The first line confirms the package's promise in other words. The viewer clicked for that promise; show them they
  are in the right place.
- By 0:05 the viewer knows the question.
- By 0:20 to 0:30 they know why it matters to them, and that the answer is coming, with its shape ("three causes and
  one test").
- Show the thing, do not describe it: open on the cupped board, the failed joint, the result.
- Open a loop you will close later. Never a fake one.

## Banned openers

"Welcome back", "hey guys", "in this video", "today we're going to", "before we start", "make sure to subscribe",
"my name is". A logo sting. A long cold open that does not match the title. `script_lint.py` blocks the first five.

## Reading the result

| What you see at 0:30 | Likely cause | Fix next time |
|---|---|---|
| a cliff in the first 10 s | the first frame or line does not match the thumbnail | open on the thumbnail's subject |
| a steady fall from 0:10 to 0:30 | the opening wanders before the question | state the question earlier |
| good R30, then a fall at 0:45 | the "minimum world" block is too long | cut it to what the next block needs |

Compare R30 only with your own videos of similar length. There is no universal good number.
