# Channel strategy reference

## Business goal decides the metric

| Model | Primary metric | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ads (YouTube Partner Program) | watch hours, revenue per thousand views | entry needs 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months, or 10M Shorts views in 90 days (check the current YouTube help page, the rules change); revenue per view varies a lot by niche |
| Sponsors | steady views per video in a niche, audience fit | sponsors look at the last 10 videos, not the best one |
| Services or B2B leads | tagged visits, sign-ups, booked calls | a small channel can pay back if the viewer is the buyer |
| Own product | conversion from the description and pinned comment | the video is the demo |
| Trust or brand | returning viewers, branded search | slow and real |

## Traffic sources

| Source | What wins there | Build for it |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube search | the exact question answered fast | the viewer's phrase in the first 40 characters of the title, the answer inside 30 s, chapters |
| Browse (home feed) | curiosity and a promise in one glance | a strong thumbnail concept, a topic broad enough for cold viewers |
| Suggested | being the natural next video after a popular one | topics next to big videos in the niche, packaging in the same language |
| External | being useful where the question is asked | embeds in posts that already rank, forum answers, docs links |

CTR differs a lot by source. Search CTR is usually far higher than browse CTR. Compare only within one source.

## The potential model

Potential is roughly audience interest x clarity of the promise x quality of viewing x desire to come back.
Where to look for each factor:

- Interest: neighbour outliers, quoted questions (topic-finder).
- Clarity: CTR within one traffic source, the stranger test (packaging-lab).
- Viewing: 30-second hold, the retention curve (retention-script, analytics-loop).
- Coming back: returning viewers, series grammar, playlists.

## Channel page (after about six videos)

- Banner: who it is for and what they get, readable on a phone.
- Trailer or featured video: the best performer.
- Description: the viewer's question in the first line, then what the channel does, then one link.
- Playlists: one per series, best video first. Name them with search phrases. They double as learning paths.
- Links: one primary offer at the top.

## Pacing

A rhythm you can hold for six months beats a burst. More uploads spread your impressions thinner if quality drops.
